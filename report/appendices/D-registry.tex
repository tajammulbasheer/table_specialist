\chapter{Run Registry}\label{app:registry}

Run identifiers follow the scheme in \cref{sec:runids}. Training-run directories (\fpath{runs/train_<arm>_<profile>_seed<s>/round<r>/})
hold the questions, programs, validation verdicts (\fpath{validated.jsonl}), training examples, adapters, logs and a
round summary. Test cells hold per-case predictions (\fpath{runs/<cell>/preds.jsonl}) and a summary in
\fpath{results/baseline/<cell>.json}. Training runs are also logged to a local MLflow store (\fpath{mlruns/}).
